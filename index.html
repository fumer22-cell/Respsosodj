How to drive

Imagine an invisible string tied to the back of your car. Touch anywhere — that spot is where you grabbed it. Keep your finger down and pull:

  • Up — throttle. Further = more.
  • Down — brake. Hold it at a standstill to reverse.
  • Left / right — steer. Further = more lock.
  • Quick flick (or spin your finger in a circle) — handbrake, to kick the car into a drift.
  • Sharp flick down — emergency brake.
  • Let go — coast. The car idles forward slowly.

Listen to your co-driver: “Left 3, long, don't cut” means a medium left-hander that goes on a while with rocks on the inside. 1 is slowest, 6 is nearly flat out; HP is a hairpin.

Stay on the road: more than a few seconds off it and you're put back at the last checkpoint with a time penalty.
