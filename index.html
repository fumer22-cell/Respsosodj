How to drive

Imagine an invisible string tied to your car. Touch anywhere and keep your finger down: that spot is where you grabbed it, and you're instantly on full throttle.

  • Pull back a little: lift off the throttle and brake lightly.
  • Pull back hard: hard braking. Keep holding at a standstill to reverse.
  • Drag left / right: steer. Further means more lock.
  • HANDBRAKE button (second thumb): kick the tail out. While drifting, your steering sets the drift angle and throttle keeps it going.
  • Let go: coast.

Drifting scores points. Hold a slide to build a combo; hitting something loses it.

The co-driver calls corners: “Left 3, long, don't cut”. 1 is slowest, 6 is nearly flat out, HP is a hairpin. More than a few seconds off the road puts you back at the last checkpoint with a time penalty.
